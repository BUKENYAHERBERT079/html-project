AFFORESTATION

REFERS TO THE PROCESS OF PLANTING TREES WHERE THERE WERE NO PREVIOUS FORESTS,ITS A KEY STRATEGY IN COMBATING CLIMATE CHANGE AND PROMOTING BIODIVERSITY'UNLIKE REFORESTATION.WHICH INVOLOVES REPLANTING TREES IN AREAS THAT WERE PREVIOUSLY FORESTED BUT HAVEBEEN CUT DOWN OR DEGRADED
AFFORESTATAION IS DONE ON LAND THAT HAS FORESTED FOR LONG TIME OR EVER.

REFORASTATION

THE PROCESS OF PLANTING TREES IN AN AREA THAT WAS PREVIOUSLY FORESTED BUT HAS BEEN DEGRADED OR DEFORESTED UNLIKE AFFORESATION WHICH INVOLVES PLANTING TREES IN AREAS
THAT WERENOT PREVIOULY FORESTS,REFORESTATION FOCUSES ON RESTORING LOST FORESTS TO RECOVER ECOSYSTEMS AND ENVIRONMENTAL BALANCES.
